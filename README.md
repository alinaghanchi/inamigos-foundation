## 📌 Project Overview

The InAmigos Foundation Website is a modern and responsive web platform developed to strengthen the digital presence of InAmigos Foundation. The platform showcases the organization's mission, initiatives, community programs, volunteer opportunities, and social impact.

The website aims to improve public engagement, increase awareness, and provide a centralized platform for stakeholders, volunteers, and beneficiaries.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Enhance NGO digital presence
- Increase volunteer participation
- Showcase foundation initiatives
- Improve community outreach
- Present impact and achievements
- Create an accessible information platform

---

### 🏠 Homepage
